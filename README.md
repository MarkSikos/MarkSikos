# Hi, I'm Mark 👋

PhD student in Computer Science at the **University of Manchester**, working on
**representation learning for asynchronous multimodal data** – how neural networks can learn
shared, transferable representations across modalities that arrive at different times, rates and
granularities, with a focus on self-supervised and multimodal objectives.

- 🎓 **PhD** Computer Science – University of Manchester
- 🎓 **MSc** Autonomous Systems – ELTE, Budapest · thesis: *Neural Approximation of Affine Correspondences*
- 🎓 **BSc** Computer Science, minor in Mathematics – ELTE, Budapest
- 🔬 Interests: representation learning, self-supervised & multimodal learning, sequence and time-series models
